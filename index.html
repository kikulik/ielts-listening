    Before you start

    • Use headphones and a quiet room. Each test takes about 30 minutes; you cannot pause it in exam mode.
    • Spelling counts. Capitals don’t. Keep to the word limit: “ONE WORD ONLY” means one word.
    • The band score uses the usual IELTS Listening conversion of your mark out of 40. It is an estimate: the real conversion moves by a mark or so between test versions.
    • These are original practice tests in the IELTS format, not official IELTS material, and this site has no connection with IELTS, the British Council, IDP or Cambridge. The recordings use computer voices.
